# 7-important-pivot-tables-in-MIS-data-finance-analysts
# Excel Business Intelligence Project – 7 Important Pivot Table Reports

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel Pivot Tables can be used to transform raw transactional sales data into meaningful business insights.

Using a retail sales dataset, I created seven business-focused Pivot Table reports that answer common analytical questions faced by Data Analysts and MIS Analysts.

The objective of this project was to practice data summarization, KPI reporting, performance analysis, and business decision support using Microsoft Excel.

---

## Dataset Information

The dataset contains retail sales transactions with the following fields:

- Order Date
- Order ID
- Customer Name
- City
- Product
- Category
- Units Sold
- Revenue
- Cost
- Target Revenue

Additional calculated fields include:

- Quarter
- Month Index
- Month Name

# Reports Created

## 1. Customer Retention Analysis

Objective

Identify repeat customers and evaluate customer retention.

Business Insight

- Customers with multiple purchases can be identified easily.
- Helps businesses understand repeat purchasing behavior.
- Useful for customer loyalty analysis.

---

## 2. Quarterly Performance Analysis

Objective

Compare quarterly sales performance.

Metrics Used

- Total Revenue
- Number of Orders
- Target Revenue
- Profit

Business Value

Helps management identify the strongest and weakest business quarters.

---

## 3. Pareto Analysis (80/20 Rule)

Objective

Identify customers generating the majority of revenue.

Metrics Used

- Revenue Contribution
- Percentage of Revenue
- Cumulative Percentage

Business Value

Highlights high-value customers responsible for a significant share of revenue.

---

## 4. Target vs Actual Analysis

Objective

Compare actual revenue against target revenue.

Business Value

Measures whether revenue targets are being achieved and helps identify performance gaps.

---

## 5. Profit Analysis

Objective

Analyze product profitability.

Metrics Used

- Revenue
- Profit
- Average Profit Percentage

Business Value

Helps identify products with strong profit margins and supports pricing decisions.

---

## 6. Product Portfolio Matrix

Objective

Evaluate products based on revenue and units sold.

Business Value

Helps classify products according to business performance and sales contribution.

---

## 7. Category Distribution

Objective

Analyze revenue contribution by product category.

Business Value

Shows which categories contribute the highest share of sales and supports inventory planning.
