Project Report
Excel Business Intelligence Report


Project Objective - 
The primary objective of this project was to analyze retail sales data using Microsoft Excel Pivot Tables and convert raw transactional data into meaningful business insights.
The project demonstrates how Pivot Tables can be used to summarize large datasets, compare performance, evaluate profitability, monitor business targets, and support strategic decision-making.


Dataset Description
The dataset consists of retail sales transactions containing customer, product, revenue, cost, and target information.
Additional date fields such as Quarter and Month were included to enable time-based analysis.


Analysis Performed
Customer Retention Analysis
Created a Pivot Table to identify customers making repeated purchases.
This analysis helps businesses understand customer loyalty and retention trends.


Quarterly Performance Analysis
A Pivot Table was developed to summarize:
Revenue
Number of Orders
Target Revenue
Profit
The report enables comparison of business performance across quarters.


Pareto Analysis
Using Pivot Tables and cumulative percentage calculations, customers were ranked according to revenue contribution.
This report demonstrates the Pareto Principle, highlighting customers responsible for the majority of business revenue.


Target vs Actual Analysis
Compared actual revenue with planned target revenue.
This analysis assists management in evaluating performance against predefined goals.


Profit Analysis
Calculated revenue, profit, and average profit percentage for individual products.
The report identifies products generating the highest profitability.

Product Portfolio Matrix
Analyzed products based on revenue and units sold.
This helps classify products according to their contribution to overall business performance.


Category Distribution
Summarized sales by product category to identify categories contributing the highest revenue.
Useful for inventory planning and category-level business strategy.
